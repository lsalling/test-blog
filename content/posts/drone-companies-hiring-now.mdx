## 2. Wingtra
Wingtra, hailing from Switzerland and spun out of ETH Zurich in 2016, specializes in vertical take-off and landing (VTOL) drones like the WingtraOne. In 2025, they've upgraded their mapping capabilities with advanced sensors, making waves in precision agriculture and urban planning. Their Swiss-engineered designs are trusted worldwide in industries like mining and environmental monitoring, offering tech company careers in flight software, data analysis, and hardware refinement. For those eyeing remote tech jobs or hands-on roles in geographic systems, Wingtra blends drone tech with practical, world-shaping applications.

Their focus on durability and practical innovation opens up tech company careers in software optimization, hardware testing, and field operations.
